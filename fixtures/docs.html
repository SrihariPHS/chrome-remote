Year 10 Science — Big Ideas

Every living thing is made of cells, and every cell is a small chemical factory that needs energy, matter and instructions.

Forces change motion. A net force is the sum of all forces acting on an object, and it is measured in newtons.

Energy is conserved. It moves between stores — kinetic, gravitational, chemical, thermal — but is never created or destroyed.
